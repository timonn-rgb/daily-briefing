Analysis

US stocks rose Friday after a weaker-than-expected September jobs report eased concerns about another Federal Reserve rate hike this month. The S&P 500 gained 0.73%, the Nasdaq Composite rose 1.19% to an intraday record led by Nvidia, and the Dow added 0.49%. Nonfarm payrolls increased by just 29,000, versus a forecast of 84,000, and the unemployment rate rose to 4.2%. Even so, the 10-year Treasury yield rose 4 basis points to 5.28%, extending this week's bond selloff, while the VIX fell 6.59% to 15.31.

In Asia, the Nikkei fell 0.94% and the Hang Seng dropped 2.60%, while the Shanghai Composite edged up 0.31%. European markets were higher, with the DAX up 1.17%, the Euro Stoxx 50 up 1.02% and the FTSE 100 up 0.32%. Oil fell after G7 nations agreed to release up to 100 million barrels of diesel and crude reserves over four months, with WTI down 1.90% and Brent down 0.06%; Trump said he would not impose a threatened ban on US diesel exports.

No economic data releases or watch-list earnings are scheduled today. Markets will watch Brazil's presidential election on Sunday and Bosnia's vote on October 4, along with ongoing tensions following North Korea's latest missile test and the US military buildup in the Middle East.

Geopolitics

North Korea fires ballistic missile amid rising tension with South Korea

North Korea fired a ballistic missile toward the sea off its east coast on Saturday, which flew more than 700km according to South Korea's military. The launch adds to tension with South Korea following border landmine blasts last month. Japan also confirmed the missile.

Why it matters: Repeated missile tests keep regional security risk elevated for South Korea, Japan and the US.

Sources: AP News · Bloomberg Politics · Al Jazeera · Reuters · DW

US sends third carrier group as Iran tensions and Yemen fighting intensify

The US is sending a third aircraft carrier strike group to the Middle East as Trump weighs resuming strikes on Iran after the midterms. Separately, Iran jailed a hard-line lawmaker who had publicly opposed a diplomatic deal with the US to end the war, exposing rifts within Iran's leadership. Fighting has also intensified in Yemen, with Yemen's army reporting 474 attacks in 24 hours that killed or wounded 1,540 Houthi fighters, and Sanaa was bombarded overnight.

Why it matters: An escalation involving Iran, Yemen and a larger US military presence raises the risk of wider regional conflict and could affect oil shipping routes and prices.

Sources: CNBC Top News · NYT World · Al Jazeera · NYT World

Two Iranians charged in UK over alleged plot targeting Jewish community

Two Iranian nationals were charged in the UK with terrorism offenses over an alleged plot to target Manchester's Jewish community, with police saying a third man, likely in Iran, was in contact with them around Yom Kippur. Separately, Joshua Kerry, the man accused of murdering former UK lawmaker Ann Widdecombe, was charged with preparing terrorist acts, including a plot against Reform UK's Nigel Farage. A local police chief said the case shows the need for reform to help small forces handle such threats.

Why it matters: The charges point to continued domestic terrorism risk in the UK tied to foreign state actors and add pressure on UK counter-terrorism policing resources.

Sources: Guardian World · Bloomberg Politics · NYT World · FT World · Reuters · Al Jazeera · FT World · FT World · NYT World · DW · Reuters · Guardian World

Brazil heads to the polls Sunday in Lula-Bolsonaro rematch

Brazil holds a presidential election Sunday, a rematch between President Lula da Silva and Jair Bolsonaro's son Flavio, four years after Lula narrowly defeated the elder Bolsonaro. Lula held his final campaign rally in Rio, saying he is the only thing standing between Brazil and a "fascist" resurgence, while crime has emerged as a top concern for voters. The US and Australia suspended consular and diplomatic operations in Brazil ahead of the vote, citing security concerns, and Brazilian police served warrants over an alleged threat to US diplomatic sites.

Why it matters: The outcome will shape Latin America's largest economy and its foreign policy alignment at a time of broader rightward political shifts across the region.

Economy & Policy

G7 agrees to release 100 million barrels of oil and diesel reserves

G7 nations agreed to release up to 100 million barrels of oil and diesel from emergency reserves over the next four months through the IEA, after President Trump threatened to ban US diesel exports amid record fuel prices in Europe. Trump later said he would not go ahead with the export ban following the coordinated release. Oil prices fell after the announcement, with WTI crude down 1.90% and Brent down 0.06%. UK diesel prices had climbed to a record £2 a litre before the announcement.

Why it matters: The release aims to head off further fuel-price spikes that have been squeezing European economies and adding to inflation pressure ahead of the US midterms.

Sources: BBC World · CNBC Top News · Guardian World · NYT World · NYT World · Al Jazeera · Bloomberg Politics · Reuters · Reuters · AP News · Bloomberg Politics · FT Markets · MarketWatch · NPR World · CNBC Top News · Reuters · Reuters

US jobs growth stalls sharply in September

US nonfarm payrolls rose by just 29,000 in September, well below the 84,000 forecast, and the unemployment rate rose to 4.2%, the Bureau of Labor Statistics said. The weak report reduced the odds traders see of a Federal Reserve rate hike this month, though one report said the Fed may skip October but still hike in December. US stocks rose on the data, with the S&P 500 up 0.73% and the Nasdaq up 1.19% to an intraday record. The 10-year Treasury yield still rose 4 basis points to 5.28%, extending a weekly bond selloff.

Why it matters: The jobs slowdown feeds into the debate over whether the Fed will raise rates again this year, a central question for bond and equity markets.

Sources: CNBC Top News · CNBC Top News · CNBC Top News · CNBC Top News · Bloomberg Markets · Bloomberg Politics · Reuters · AP News · Bloomberg Politics · FT Markets · Reuters · NYT Business · NYT Business

Paramount-Warner Bros. merger to be named Skydance

David Ellison, who will run the combined company, said the merged Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery will be named Skydance. Warner Bros.' chief executives were told on Friday that they would not stay on once the merger completes.

Why it matters: The renaming signals how Ellison intends to reshape leadership and branding across one of the media industry's largest mergers.

FlyDubai co-pilot stabs captain mid-flight; plane lands safely

A co-pilot stabbed the captain of FlyDubai flight 1073, bound for Tel Aviv, before passengers and two off-duty pilots overpowered him and the plane landed safely in Saudi Arabia. The attacker had reportedly been barred from flying in his native Oman over extremism concerns. The wounded captain, hailed as a hero, described the incident to India's prime minister, and Israeli officials say they plan to question the co-pilot. The attack has raised questions about airline pilot background checks and mental-health screening.

Why it matters: The incident highlights gaps in international aviation security screening and could prompt new scrutiny of pilot vetting standards industry-wide.

Sources: Guardian World · AP News · Guardian World · DW · AP News · AP News · NYT World · Al Jazeera · Al Jazeera · Reuters · Reuters · Reuters · NYT World · DW · NYT World

Student protests spread across France

Student protests have spread across France, with students setting fires at some schools and clashing with police for over two weeks. Reports put the number of affected schools at around 735 to more than 1,000, with roughly 2,000 arrests and dozens injured. Students are protesting overloaded timetables, staffing shortages and poor facilities, and have called for further strikes despite a government-proposed "day of dialogue."

Why it matters: Sustained, large-scale unrest in France's school system adds to broader political pressure on the government and could spill into wider social tension.
