Markets rally despite Nvidia scandal as Ukraine and Iran tensions ease
Daily Briefing · Tuesday, 10 November 2026

AT A GLANCE
- Wall Street rallied sharply Monday, with the S&P 500, Nasdaq and Dow all up more than 2%, recovering most of last week's losses.
- Nvidia's board is investigating CEO Jensen Huang after he admitted an affair with a woman alleged to be a Chinese spy; shares fell and shareholders sued.
- Trump said the war with Iran is 'effectively over' and further strikes are unlikely, helping push oil prices lower.
- Russia, Ukraine and the US resumed trilateral peace talks in Abu Dhabi, with expectations for a breakthrough described as low.
- China's trade surplus topped $1 trillion for a second straight year as October exports surged 35%.
