- Weekend riots rock France as unrest spreads across Europe: The unrest adds political pressure on Macron ahead of a critical budget vote and comes as several European countries prepare for elections next year, raising concerns about wider social instability tied to living costs.
- Putin proposes Trump-Xi summit on Ukraine as Washington-Kyiv rift deepens: A split between Washington and its European allies over Russia policy, combined with intensifying strikes on energy infrastructure, adds uncertainty for oil markets and the diplomatic path to ending the war.
- China's economy accelerates to 4.5% growth in third quarter: Stronger-than-expected Chinese growth driven mainly by exports underlines Beijing's reliance on overseas demand while domestic spending stays weak, with implications for global trade and goods prices.
- Indonesian stocks slump to six-year low on MSCI downgrade threat: A downgrade would likely trigger further outflows from passive funds tracking emerging-market indexes, adding pressure on Indonesia's currency and borrowing costs.
- AI trade wobbles again as Asian chip stocks extend losses: Renewed doubts about AI financing and chip demand threaten to extend recent volatility in global tech and semiconductor stocks, a sector that has driven much of this year's market gains.

- Fire at Canadian military base kills four, injures dozens: The tragedy is among the deadliest peacetime incidents for Canada's military and is likely to prompt scrutiny of training safety protocols.
- Trump endorses Netanyahu as West Bank settler violence draws US condemnation: The endorsement and the condemnation of settler violence highlight tensions within the US-Israel relationship just a week before a closely watched election.
