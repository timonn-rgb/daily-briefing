TOP STORIES
- Alphabet shares sink 10% as AI spending concerns rattle tech: As one of the largest AI spenders, Alphabet's results raise doubts about whether heavy AI capital expenditure across the tech sector will generate sufficient returns, a question weighing on global equity markets.
- Microsoft jumps on Azure growth despite 60,000 buyout offers: Microsoft's results show it containing AI infrastructure costs while still growing, offering a contrasting signal to investors worried about AI spending after Alphabet's earnings.
- Fed holds rates steady; Warsh signals changes ahead: The Fed's pause leaves policy direction into December's meeting uncertain, with markets now watching inflation data for signs of whether a rate hike is back on the table.
- Spain's second deadly train crash this year kills 68: The crash has raised questions about rail safety oversight in Spain and comes ahead of a national election, adding political pressure on the government.
- Israel's election ends in deadlock, coalition talks loom: Prolonged coalition negotiations could affect Israel's policy toward Iran and the wider region, including the possibility of renewed military action while a caretaker government is in place.
- Flávio Bolsonaro flees Brazil to US, disputes election loss: Bolsonaro's flight and fraud claims add to political instability in Brazil, Latin America's largest economy, while he faces multiple domestic investigations.
- UK Budget raises taxes on banks and landlords for £100 handouts: The tax increases and household support shape the UK's fiscal position and consumer spending power, a factor markets watch alongside the Bank of England's policy path.
- North Korea fires about 10 ballistic missiles toward the sea: Repeated North Korean missile tests keep regional security tensions elevated, a region the Fed's chairman has cited as a leading indicator for global supply chains.
- Jio Platforms' blockbuster debut fuels India IPO pipeline: A successful mega-listing could encourage more large Indian firms to pursue IPOs, deepening the country's capital markets.
