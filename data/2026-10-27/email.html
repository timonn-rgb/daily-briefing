Anthropic files for IPO, warning of AI's 'existential risks'
The IPO is a test of whether AI valuations can be sustained after recent doubts about the AI trade, with ripple effects already visible in chip, semiconductor and optical stocks across US and Asian markets.

Israelis vote in first election since the October 7 attacks
The result will determine whether Netanyahu remains in power after three years of regional war and could shape Israel's policy toward Iran, Gaza and the West Bank.

Lula wins Brazil's presidency; currency suffers worst day in 25 years
The result bucks a rightward political shift across Latin America and raises market concern about Brazil's fiscal trajectory, while denying Trump a regional ally.

Oil falls as diplomatic efforts ease Middle East supply fears
Lower oil prices ease cost pressure on consumers and businesses globally, including transit operators that had faced higher fuel bills from the Iran war.

Microsoft offers buyouts to 60,000 employees in AI efficiency push
The move signals that even leading AI investors are tightening costs elsewhere, a sign of the financial strain large-scale AI spending is placing on big tech balance sheets.

Meta plans $95 billion stock offering to fund AI expansion
The scale of the fundraising underscores how capital-intensive the AI buildout has become, and highlights a widening gap between well-funded giants and smaller AI firms.

DoorDash in talks to buy Instacart for about $17 billion

Bank of Japan expected to hold rates but signal hawkish shift
