AI-driven global sell-off deepens as Democrats' midterm sweep rattles markets

At a glance

  • Wall Street's AI-stock rout deepened overnight, with the S&P 500 down 4.36% and the Nasdaq down 5.52% in their worst session since April 2025.
  • Asian markets extended the sell-off Friday: Japan's Nikkei 225 entered a bear market and South Korean stocks hit a nine-month low.
  • Democrats' sweep of the US midterms raised fears of tighter AI and data-center regulation, a key driver of the tech-stock slide.
  • Hoax bomb threats disrupted dozens of flights across Europe this week, forcing emergency landings and diversions.
  • US October jobs data and a speech by Bank of Japan Governor Ueda are due later today, alongside continuing Iran-US tensions.
