AI-driven global sell-off deepens as Democrats' midterm sweep rattles markets
Daily Briefing · Friday, 06 November 2026

AT A GLANCE
- Wall Street's AI-stock rout deepened overnight, with the S&P 500 down 4.36% and the Nasdaq down 5.52% in their worst session since April 2025.
- Asian markets extended the sell-off Friday: Japan's Nikkei 225 entered a bear market and South Korean stocks hit a nine-month low.
- Democrats' sweep of the US midterms raised fears of tighter AI and data-center regulation, a key driver of the tech-stock slide.
- Hoax bomb threats disrupted dozens of flights across Europe this week, forcing emergency landings and diversions.
- US October jobs data and a speech by Bank of Japan Governor Ueda are due later today, alongside continuing Iran-US tensions.

TOP STORIES
- Global stock sell-off deepens as AI trade unwinds: A sustained unwind of the AI trade would hit the valuations that have driven much of this year's global equity gains and could ripple into financing for the broader data-center buildout.
- Democrats sweep US midterms, flipping control of Congress: A Democratic-controlled Congress is expected to launch investigations into the administration and could complicate Trump's legislative agenda for the remainder of his term.
- Democrats' win fuels fears of tighter AI data-center rules: Tighter regulation or financing scrutiny of data centers could slow the infrastructure buildout that has underpinned AI-related corporate spending and stock valuations.
- Lawmakers summon Nvidia's Huang over China spying concerns: Scrutiny of Nvidia's China ties adds a geopolitical risk for the company whose chips are central to the global AI buildout.
- Iran-US tensions keep oil markets on edge: A further escalation between the US and Iran could disrupt oil supply through the Gulf and push energy prices sharply higher.
- Hoax bomb threats disrupt dozens of European flights: Repeated, unexplained disruption to European air travel raises security concerns for passengers and infrastructure across the continent.
- Bank of England raises interest rates to 4%: A higher UK policy rate affects borrowing costs for households and businesses and is being announced amid an ongoing cost-of-living crisis.
- Hong Kong activist Joshua Wong sentenced to 10 more years: The sentence is a further sign of the tightening legal crackdown on Hong Kong's pro-democracy movement.
- Paraguay's president resigns amid bank-collapse scandal: The collapse of the government adds political instability to a South American economy already dealing with a major banking failure.
- Trump says Russia-Ukraine deal is 'near' after meeting Putin envoy: Any US-brokered deal would shape the future of the war and European security, though the lack of Ukrainian involvement in the latest talks raises questions about the terms on the table.
