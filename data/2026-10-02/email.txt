AT A GLANCE

TOP STORIES
- US sends third aircraft carrier and thousands more troops toward Iran: A larger US military presence near Iran raises the risk of a wider regional conflict and has already pushed up oil prices, a key input for global inflation.
- FlyDubai cockpit attack under investigation: The incident has heightened security concerns around flights linked to Israel and fed into wider regional tensions following the Iran conflict.
- South Korea and Ukraine clash over North Korean POW transfer: The dispute strains ties between two US allies at a time when South Korea is also weighing deeper economic and military cooperation with Washington.
- Brazil heads into tight presidential election: The outcome will shape policy direction in Latin America's largest economy and is being watched for signs of foreign interference in the vote.
- Trump threatens US diesel export ban, pressures Europe: An export ban or reserve drawdown would ripple through global fuel markets and could raise costs for European consumers and businesses already facing high energy prices.
- Global bond sell-off pushes US yields to highest since 2002: Persistently higher borrowing costs raise financing costs for governments and companies worldwide and have been a key swing factor for stocks in recent sessions.
- France's political and fiscal crisis deepens: France is the eurozone's second-largest economy, and sustained investor flight from its debt could add to broader pressure on European bond and currency markets.
- US September jobs report due today: The jobs data will inform the Federal Reserve's next policy moves and is a key political data point heading into the midterms.
- Nike posts disappointing sales, announces more layoffs: Nike's struggles are a bellwether for consumer discretionary spending and for the broader sporting-goods and footwear sector's exposure to China.
- AI infrastructure financing comes under scrutiny: Questions about how AI infrastructure spending is financed could affect valuations across the technology sector and the credit markets funding the buildout.
