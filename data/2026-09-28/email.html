Top stories

Trump rejects Iran's Hormuz proposal, sending oil and bond yields higher
A prolonged Hormuz standoff threatens to keep oil and shipping costs elevated and adds to inflation and borrowing-cost pressure just as central banks weigh their next moves.

UK investigates possible Iran link to bomb plot at US-used air base
An attack on a base used for US strikes against Iran would mark a significant escalation of the conflict onto British soil and could harden Western policy toward Tehran.

US and China detail deal to cut tariffs on $60 billion of goods
The partial tariff rollback eases some trade tension between the world's two largest economies but leaves major agricultural and energy sticking points unresolved.

Nvidia announces $150bn buyback and new AI agent safety platform
The buyback signals Nvidia's confidence despite a slowdown in share gains, while the safety platform responds to growing concern about AI agents operating with less oversight.

UK diesel prices hit record high amid Iran-driven supply crunch
Sustained high diesel costs raise expenses for hauliers, farmers and industry across Europe, adding to inflation pressure while central banks are already contending with higher energy prices.

France's far-right National Rally wins record Senate seats
The gains strengthen the far right's institutional foothold in France months before a presidential election that could reshape the country's, and the EU's, political direction.

Mass shootings in South Africa leave 28 dead
The killings underscore South Africa's ongoing struggle with armed violence ahead of municipal elections on November 4, when local policing is expected to be a central issue.

Australia's Northern Star rejects $27bn takeover bid from Gold Fields
The rejected bid highlights continued consolidation interest in the gold mining sector as elevated gold prices draw takeover activity.

Serbia's President Vucic resigns to run for prime minister
The move is an unusual attempt by an entrenched leader to hold onto power through a different office, and its outcome will shape Serbia's political stability and its path toward EU accession.

UK housebuilder shares surge on new 'Your First Home' scheme
Government support for first-time buyers could boost housebuilding activity and provide a rare bright spot for UK consumer-facing stocks amid broader market pressure from rising yields and oil prices.
