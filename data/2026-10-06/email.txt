AT A GLANCE

TOP STORIES
- Treasury yields hit 2002 highs as bond sell-off deepens: Higher borrowing costs raise financing risks for heavily indebted companies and governments and could pressure equity valuations if yields keep climbing.
- France's debt sell-off and school protests deepen before election: Persistent French fiscal and political instability is a source of contagion risk for eurozone bond markets and the euro.
- Bolsonaro surge sets up Brazil runoff, boosts markets: A rightward shift in Latin America's largest economy would reshape the region's politics and is already driving a sharp rally in Brazilian markets.
- Quebec separatists win election, reviving independence push: A renewed independence push in Quebec adds a new source of political uncertainty for Canada as it navigates economic pressure from the US.
- Saudi-backed forces push Houthis back from key Red Sea strait: Fighting around the Bab al-Mandab strait threatens a critical corridor for global oil and shipping traffic.
- US pulls B-1 bombers from UK base over threat concerns: Unexplained military repositioning and gaps in congressional briefings raise questions about transparency around an active US-Iran conflict.
- AI-led tech rally pushes Wall Street to fresh records: Markets are increasingly dependent on a narrow set of AI-related stocks to offset the drag from rising bond yields.
- AI safety concerns intensify after Australia hacking incident: Real-world hacking incidents tied to AI agents are accelerating calls for binding safety regulation of the industry.
- Banks launch record $60bn financing for Broadcom-Anthropic chip deal: A financing package of this size highlights both the capital intensity of the AI buildout and growing concerns about AI-related debt risk.
- Russia downplays plague death as Trump offers US help: A disease outbreak at a Russian research institute, even if contained, is being watched for signs of wider public health risk.
