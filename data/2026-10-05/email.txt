TOP STORIES
- Brazil's election heads to an October 25 runoff: A Bolsonaro win would mark a sharp rightward shift in Latin America's largest economy and investors are positioning for Brazilian assets to move sharply on the result, while tension with the Supreme Court could intensify.
- Yemen's government launches major offensive against Houthis: Escalation in Yemen risks wider disruption to Middle East shipping and energy infrastructure, adding a new source of regional instability alongside the ongoing US-Iran standoff.
- US pulls all bombers from UK base after suspected Iran-linked terror plot: The episode shows how the US-Iran confrontation is spilling into Europe through smaller-scale, hard-to-attribute attacks, raising security concerns at Western military installations.
- OPEC+ holds output steady as Saudi Arabia cuts Asia oil prices to six-year low: Falling oil prices ease inflation pressure for consumers but point to intensifying competition among major producers for market share.
- Treasury yields stay elevated as markets await Fed minutes: Elevated borrowing costs raise questions about fiscal sustainability and could pressure equity valuations if yields keep climbing.
- Euro falls to 17-month low on political and fiscal risk: A weaker euro reflects deepening concern over European political and fiscal stability and raises import costs for the eurozone.
- Trump names intelligence chief to lead new AI task force: Placing AI oversight under the intelligence chief signals the administration is approaching AI primarily as a national security issue, which could shape future regulation.
- FBI arrests woman accused of spying for China on Taiwan leader's son: The case highlights ongoing espionage concerns tied to Taiwan and is likely to add friction to US-China relations.
- Nationalist Dodik claims victory in Bosnia election: A strengthened secessionist movement in Bosnia could reignite ethnic and political tensions in the Balkans and complicate the region's path toward EU integration.
- Pension funds trim US equities over AI concentration risk: Reduced pension fund demand for US equities, if it spreads, could weigh on a market that has been heavily reliant on AI-related gains.
