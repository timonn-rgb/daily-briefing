OpenAI revenue miss rattles AI stocks; Iran tensions keep oil, yields in focus

At a glance

  • OpenAI's ~$50bn annualized revenue, below prior signals, triggered the Nasdaq's worst one-day drop since mid-August and hit Nvidia, Oracle and CoreWeave.
  • Trump said the US will not attack Iran before November's midterms, even as Houthi missiles hit Riyadh airport and Hormuz tanker attacks continued.
  • Russian strikes killed more than 30 people on Ukrainian buses this week; US, European and Ukrainian officials meet in Miami to discuss new peace proposals.
  • The US suspended Microsoft, Adobe and several Indian IT firms from a green-card labor program amid a visa-abuse crackdown.
  • Nvidia-backed Firmus withdrew its $5bn Australian IPO, citing market volatility, as SoftBank seeks $100bn from Gulf investors to fund its AI ambitions.
