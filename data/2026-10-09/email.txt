OpenAI revenue miss rattles AI stocks; Iran tensions keep oil, yields in focus
Daily Briefing · Friday, 09 October 2026

AT A GLANCE
- OpenAI's ~$50bn annualized revenue, below prior signals, triggered the Nasdaq's worst one-day drop since mid-August and hit Nvidia, Oracle and CoreWeave.
- Trump said the US will not attack Iran before November's midterms, even as Houthi missiles hit Riyadh airport and Hormuz tanker attacks continued.
- Russian strikes killed more than 30 people on Ukrainian buses this week; US, European and Ukrainian officials meet in Miami to discuss new peace proposals.
- The US suspended Microsoft, Adobe and several Indian IT firms from a green-card labor program amid a visa-abuse crackdown.
- Nvidia-backed Firmus withdrew its $5bn Australian IPO, citing market volatility, as SoftBank seeks $100bn from Gulf investors to fund its AI ambitions.

TOP STORIES
- Trump rules out Iran strikes before midterms as Gulf tensions escalate: Continued attacks on shipping and airports in the Gulf region keep energy markets exposed to supply disruption even as Washington signals it wants to avoid a wider war before the midterms.
- Russian strikes kill dozens in Ukraine as US, Europe meet for new peace push: A new round of talks, paired with continued deadly strikes, could shape whether the war moves toward a negotiated settlement or further escalation.
- OpenAI revenue disclosure triggers worst Nasdaq day since August: The reaction shows how sensitive markets have become to any sign that AI spending or revenue growth is slowing, after months of heavy investment in the sector.
- Bond market jitters ease after strong 30-year auction, but Pimco warns on 6% yields: Sustained high US borrowing costs affect global financing conditions and have already been cited as a factor in recent bank-stock weakness and tech-sector selling.
- Gaza violence continues one year into US-backed ceasefire: Continued casualties despite the formal ceasefire raise questions about the truce's durability and its humanitarian toll.
- US suspends Microsoft, Adobe and Indian IT firms from green-card program: The crackdown directly affects major Indian IT outsourcers and large US tech employers, with implications for their US staffing costs and operations.
- SoftBank seeks $100bn from Gulf investors to fund AI bet: A deal of this size would mark one of the largest AI-related capital raises to date and deepen Gulf sovereign wealth involvement in the AI buildout.
- Nvidia-backed Firmus scraps $5bn Australian AI data-centre IPO: The scrapped IPO is one of the clearest market signals yet that investor appetite for AI infrastructure bets has cooled, following the OpenAI revenue news and the wider tech-sector selloff.
- Starbucks weighs takeover approach for Chipotle: A deal of this size would be one of the largest restaurant-sector mergers in years, though analysts have raised doubts about the financial and strategic rationale.
- ICE shooting of man with child in car sparks New York backlash: The shooting intensifies political tension over federal immigration enforcement tactics in US cities ahead of the midterm elections.
