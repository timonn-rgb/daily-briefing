Daily Briefing · Saturday, 03 October 2026

AT A GLANCE
- G7 nations agreed to release up to 100 million barrels of oil and diesel reserves after Trump's export-ban threat; he later dropped the ban.

TOP STORIES
- G7 agrees to release 100 million barrels of oil and diesel reserves: The release aims to head off further fuel-price spikes that have been squeezing European economies and adding to inflation pressure ahead of the US midterms.
- US jobs growth stalls sharply in September: The jobs slowdown feeds into the debate over whether the Fed will raise rates again this year, a central question for bond and equity markets.
- FlyDubai co-pilot stabs captain mid-flight; plane lands safely: The incident highlights gaps in international aviation security screening and could prompt new scrutiny of pilot vetting standards industry-wide.
- North Korea fires ballistic missile amid rising tension with South Korea: Repeated missile tests keep regional security risk elevated for South Korea, Japan and the US.
- US sends third carrier group as Iran tensions and Yemen fighting intensify: An escalation involving Iran, Yemen and a larger US military presence raises the risk of wider regional conflict and could affect oil shipping routes and prices.
- Two Iranians charged in UK over alleged plot targeting Jewish community: The charges point to continued domestic terrorism risk in the UK tied to foreign state actors and add pressure on UK counter-terrorism policing resources.
- Brazil heads to the polls Sunday in Lula-Bolsonaro rematch: The outcome will shape Latin America's largest economy and its foreign policy alignment at a time of broader rightward political shifts across the region.
- Paramount-Warner Bros. merger to be named Skydance: The renaming signals how Ellison intends to reshape leadership and branding across one of the media industry's largest mergers.
- Anthropic IPO filing flags government-relations risk; firm invests in AI training: As a major AI company approaches a public listing, its risk disclosures and hiring investments offer insight into the political and talent dynamics shaping a sector that has driven much of this year's stock market gains.
- Student protests spread across France: Sustained, large-scale unrest in France's school system adds to broader political pressure on the government and could spill into wider social tension.
