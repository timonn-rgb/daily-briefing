TOP STORIES
- Nvidia board weighs ousting CEO Jensen Huang: Huang has been the public face of the AI boom and an opponent of strict chip export controls to China; his departure would create a leadership vacuum at the roughly $4 trillion company and could unsettle the AI trade that has driven much of this year's market gains.
- AI trade whipsaws as CoreWeave rally gives way to Nvidia selloff: The swing shows how closely broad market sentiment has become tied to a handful of AI-related stocks, meaning a leadership shock at Nvidia can move indexes well beyond the chip sector.
- Russia and Ukraine agree prisoner swap as peace talks end without breakthrough: A prisoner swap offers a rare concrete result after months of inconclusive talks, but the absence of a breakthrough and Ukraine's worsening power crisis underline how far a durable settlement remains.
- Spain's first general strike in 14 years turns violent: The unrest highlights how housing affordability has become a flashpoint in major European economies, with elections now looming as a test of public anger.
- Israeli strikes on Gaza hospital kill at least six: Repeated strikes on hospitals raise questions about the durability of the Gaza cease-fire and could further strain humanitarian conditions in the territory.
- Iran and Israel tensions escalate amid talk of a strike: A renewed escalation between Israel and Iran, layered on unresolved talks, raises the risk of a wider regional conflict that could again disrupt oil markets and shipping through the Gulf.
- Democrat Scott Colom's Mississippi Senate win widens Democratic majority: The result further narrows Republican control of the Senate, a shift that could affect the pace of confirmations and legislation during the lame-duck session and beyond.
- EU and UK set to delay 10% tariff on electric cars: A delay would ease near-term cost pressure on European automakers already facing competition from Chinese EV makers, though it leaves the underlying trade dispute unresolved.
- France backs Spain's candidate for ECB presidency: The outcome of the ECB leadership race will shape the direction of euro-area monetary policy for years, a matter of direct relevance to a Germany-based reader.
- US Treasury Secretary relocates to military base over Iran threat: A direct threat against a senior US official underscores how personal and acute tensions with Iran have become, even as diplomatic talks continue.
