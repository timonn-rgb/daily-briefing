Tech-led selloff deepens on AI fears as Europe's weekend protests turn violent
Daily Briefing · Sunday, 01 November 2026

AT A GLANCE
- US stocks fell sharply Friday as AI spending fears intensified; the Nasdaq dropped 2.86% and OpenAI trimmed its funding target.
- The FBI blamed North Korea for the record $6.3bn Binance hack; the exchange says it has covered 90% of losses.
- Youth-led protests over living costs turned violent in Germany and France, with rioting in Frankfurt and clashes across French cities.
- US midterm elections near with record early voting; control of Congress remains close heading into Tuesday's vote.
- Poland's Tusk vowed to press on with a contested overhaul of the constitutional court despite protests and opposition accusations.

TOP STORIES
- AI bubble fears drive deeper Wall Street selloff: A pullback in AI-related spending or valuations could ripple through the broader market given how concentrated recent gains have been in a small number of tech stocks.
- FBI blames North Korea for record $6.3bn Binance hack: The scale of the theft and its state-sponsored origin are likely to intensify scrutiny of security practices across crypto exchanges and add to pressure around North Korea's cyber activity.
- Gen Z-led protests turn violent across Germany: The unrest echoes similar youth-led protests spreading across Europe and adds pressure on Germany's government over living costs and education spending.
- France protests enter third weekend as violence flares: Repeated large-scale unrest adds political pressure on the French government, which is already facing a credit-rating setback after Fitch's outlook downgrade.
- US heads into midterm elections with record early turnout: The outcome will shape fiscal and regulatory policy, including the approach to AI and tech spending that markets have been watching closely.
- Poland's Tusk presses on with top court overhaul: The standoff over judicial independence is a central test of the rule of law in one of the EU's largest member states.
- Ukrainian evacuees from occupied Oleshky reunite with families: The evacuation highlights the humanitarian toll of Russia's occupation in southern Ukraine even as the broader war continues.
- Iran war fears persist despite renewed diplomatic push: A breakdown in the talks risks reigniting direct military conflict in the Middle East, with implications for oil markets and shipping in the Gulf.
- G20 foreign ministers end Atlanta talks without consensus: The lack of consensus underscores continuing divisions among major economies over how to handle Russia diplomatically while its war in Ukraine continues.
- Fitch downgrades France's credit outlook: A weaker credit outlook for the euro area's second-largest economy could raise its borrowing costs and add to pressure on French bond markets.
