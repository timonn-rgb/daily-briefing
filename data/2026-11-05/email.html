Democrats sweep Congress, Wall Street rallies as oil tumbles and AI stocks wobble

At a glance

  • Democrats swept control of both chambers of Congress in the US midterms, triggering Wall Street's best session since April 2025.
  • AI and data-center stocks fell on fears a Democratic Congress could slow the buildout, while hyperscalers and Apple rallied.

  • Hong Kong activist Joshua Wong was sentenced to 10 more years in prison for foreign collusion under the national security law.
  • German politician Hubert Aiwanger was found dead in a Berlin hotel; police have opened an investigation.

Top stories

Democrats sweep control of Congress
A divided Washington makes major legislative changes harder to pass and could reopen fights over the debt ceiling, taxes and tariffs, with implications that extend beyond the US.

AI and data-center stocks slide on regulatory fears, hyperscalers rally
The split signals investors are repricing which parts of the AI buildout face political risk, a rotation that could keep volatility elevated in chip and power stocks tied to data centers.

Oil extends slide as Iran war de-escalation hopes grow
Lower oil prices ease inflation pressure for importing economies and reduce one source of market volatility, but the move hinges on whether de-escalation actually follows the political signal.

Tankers catch fire in Strait of Hormuz as South Korea boosts naval presence
The Strait of Hormuz carries a large share of global oil shipments, so incidents and naval build-ups there are watched closely for their potential to disrupt energy supplies and prices.

Hong Kong activist Joshua Wong jailed for 10 more years
The sentencing underscores the continued use of the national security law against prominent dissidents and is likely to draw renewed international criticism of Hong Kong's and Beijing's human rights record.

German politician Hubert Aiwanger found dead in Berlin hotel
Aiwanger was a senior figure in Bavarian and German politics, and the unexplained death of a sitting state deputy leader is likely to draw significant domestic political attention.

Bank of England expected to raise rates for first time in three years
A rate hike would mark a shift in the BoE's policy stance after years of holding or cutting rates, with direct implications for UK borrowing costs, the pound and European market sentiment.

Israel's Herzog opens coalition talks as Netanyahu's position weakens
A change in Israel's government, combined with a less favorable US Congress, could affect the country's diplomatic and military relationships, including over the war in Gaza.

France braces for nationwide strikes amid stalled budget talks
Prolonged budget deadlock and strikes add pressure on the French government and could further delay fiscal consolidation, a point of interest for euro-area markets.
