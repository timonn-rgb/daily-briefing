Iran war drives oil and bond selloff as Anthropic files for IPO amid AI safety pause

At a glance

  • Stalled US-Iran talks pushed oil higher and Wall Street lower, with the S&P 500 down 0.77% and the 10-year yield up to 5.24%.
  • Anthropic's IPO filing disclosed an $8bn loss on $4.6bn revenue and warned its AI could pose existential risks to humanity.
  • OpenAI shelved a new model over safety concerns, extending an industry-wide pause also seen at Anthropic and in new AI legislation.
  • Nvidia announced a record $150 billion share buyback and a new platform meant to stop AI agents from acting unsafely.
  • AMD agreed to buy Fei-Fei Li's World Labs for $8.2 billion, betting on AI applied to robotics and the physical world.
