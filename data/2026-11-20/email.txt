Wall Street's AI-driven selloff deepens despite record Anthropic IPO
Daily Briefing · Friday, 20 November 2026

AT A GLANCE
- Global stocks extended their AI-driven selloff; Wall Street booked a fifth straight decline and Asian markets had their worst week in years.
- Anthropic's record $81bn IPO closed up 17% on debut but failed to revive the broader AI trade as the Nasdaq fell over 2%.
- The UN's top court ruled for the first time that a country, Myanmar, committed genocide, against the Rohingya Muslim minority.
- Trump, Xi and Putin held a first-ever trilateral summit at APEC with no breakthroughs on nuclear arms, Ukraine or AI rules.
- Nvidia faces a California subpoena and congressional scrutiny over AI chip sales to China as its stock keeps sliding.

TOP STORIES
- Anthropic's record IPO fails to lift broader AI trade: The muted market reaction beyond Anthropic's own stock suggests investors are growing more cautious about AI valuations generally, even as the listing sets a new benchmark for AI-sector IPOs.
- AI-fueled stock selloff spreads across Asia, hits multi-year lows: A sustained unwind of AI-related stock valuations could tighten financial conditions well beyond the technology sector and dent investor and consumer confidence globally.
- UN court rules Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya: It is the first time the world's top court has held a state responsible for genocide, setting a legal precedent that could shape the pending case over Gaza.
- Trump, Xi and Putin hold symbolic first trilateral summit: A former US ambassador to China warned the summit's friendly optics sent the wrong message to US allies, at a time of no concrete progress on arms control or Ukraine.
- Nvidia faces deepening scrutiny over AI chip sales to China: The scrutiny raises questions about the enforcement of US export controls on advanced AI chips and adds pressure on a company whose chips underpin much of the AI investment boom.
- US Senate passes energy permitting reform bill: Faster permitting could accelerate the energy infrastructure needed to power data centers and the broader AI buildout, though the bill still faces an uncertain path through the House.
- Kushner says Trump open to NATO-like guarantees for Ukraine: A security-guarantee framework could become a central piece of any eventual ceasefire deal, while the continuing strikes show the conflict remains unresolved.
- UK and EU close in on deal to reset post-Brexit ties: It would mark the most significant shift in UK-EU relations since Brexit, with implications for trade frictions and labor mobility between Britain and the bloc.
- Houthi attacks on Riyadh and Gulf shipping stoke oil concerns: Continued attacks on a critical Gulf shipping route raise the risk of wider regional escalation and renewed pressure on global oil supply.
- Japan's core inflation hits a yearly high as subsidies lapse: Firmer rate-hike expectations in Japan could support the yen and ripple through global bond markets already unsettled by the AI-driven stock selloff.
