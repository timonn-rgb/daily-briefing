AT A GLANCE

TOP STORIES
- Israel's president gives Eisenkot first shot at forming government: A change of government in Israel would end Netanyahu's record-long tenure and could shift the country's approach to the region at a time of high regional tension.
- Krakow shooting leaves six dead, Tusk calls it terrorism: The attack adds to a string of security incidents unsettling Europe and raises questions about far-right extremism as a driver of mass violence.
- US expands naval blockade of Iran, disables vessel in Gulf of Oman: Escalation around the Strait of Hormuz and Gulf shipping routes is a direct risk to oil supply and prices, a key variable for global markets.
- More than 50 killed in deadliest 24 hours of Yemen war: Renewed intensity in the Yemen conflict adds another front of instability in a region already on edge over Iran, with civilians bearing the heaviest toll.
- AI-trade unwind drags on Asian and global markets: A reversal in the AI trade that has driven much of this year's equity gains would have outsized effects on global indices given how concentrated markets have become in AI-related names.
- Berkshire Hathaway doubles buybacks as profit falls: The scale of buybacks signals Berkshire's own view that its stock is undervalued even as underlying earnings soften, a data point watched closely by value investors.
- New Zealand election leaves no clear winner: Prolonged coalition uncertainty could delay policy decisions in New Zealand and adds to a broader pattern of fragmented election outcomes among developed democracies this year.
- South Africa's ANC suffers worst-ever election result: A diminished ANC changes the balance of power in Africa's most industrialized economy and could reshape governance of its largest cities.
- Paraguay's ex-president charged after bank collapse: The collapse of a major bank and the fall of a sitting president underscore financial-sector fragility in Paraguay and could unsettle investor confidence in the country.
- France sees mass protests as Mélenchon launches 2027 bid: Sustained social unrest combined with a strengthening far-left challenger adds to political uncertainty in the eurozone's second-largest economy ahead of 2027.
