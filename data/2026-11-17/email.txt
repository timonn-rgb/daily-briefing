AT A GLANCE

TOP STORIES
- Nvidia leadership turmoil deepens AI stock sell-off: Nvidia is the bellwether of the AI trade; instability at the top and investor anxiety ahead of earnings could extend the broader sell-off in technology and AI-linked markets.
- US expands chip export curbs to close China's remote-access loopholes: The curbs escalate the US-China technology rivalry and add further pressure to global chip and AI-linked equities already selling off.
- Trump heads to Asia for ASEAN summit and planned Xi-Putin meeting: A first three-way summit between the US, China and Russia this year could reshape discussions on Ukraine and nuclear issues, unsettling allies wary of a great-power deal struck without them.
- Russian strikes hit Kyiv bridges as Ukraine war escalates: Strikes on critical infrastructure threaten a harsh winter for Ukrainian civilians and complicate parallel efforts to negotiate an end to the war.
- Iran tensions rise as third US carrier reaches Gulf, Houthis advance: Rising tensions near key Gulf shipping routes carry the risk of disrupting global oil supplies and further destabilizing the region's economy.
- Five EU countries announce migrant deportation hub in Rwanda: The plan marks a significant shift in EU migration policy, with Germany among the participating countries, and could set a precedent for externalizing asylum processing.
- Fed chair flags AI's economic uncertainty as a policy challenge: The Fed's read on AI's economic effects could shape future interest-rate decisions, directly affecting the market outlook.
- Developing countries' debt load swells amid high global rates: Rising developing-world debt distress threatens broader financial stability and growth prospects beyond the countries directly affected.
- US bank and airline technology outages add to disruption concerns: A string of unrelated outages across banking, aviation and software has raised questions about the resilience of critical digital infrastructure ahead of the Thanksgiving travel period.
- California sues OpenAI over Hugging Face security breach: The case could set a legal precedent for AI companies' liability when their models act autonomously beyond their intended scope.
