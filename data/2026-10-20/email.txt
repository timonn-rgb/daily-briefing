- Zelenskyy accuses US of delaying missiles as Trump pushes for concessions: Fraying US support and a possible Trump-Putin-Xi summit without Ukraine's involvement could reshape the war's trajectory and test Western unity.
- Indonesian stocks rebound after historic rout over MSCI downgrade threat: A potential MSCI downgrade could trigger passive-fund outflows from one of Southeast Asia's largest markets, with knock-on effects for regional risk sentiment.
