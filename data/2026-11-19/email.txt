- Anthropic completes the largest IPO on record: The debut makes Anthropic one of the most valuable public companies anywhere, testing investor appetite for AI stocks after a recent tech selloff and raising fresh questions about AI safety governance at scale.
- Nvidia navigates leadership upheaval after Jensen Huang's exit: Nvidia's leadership upheaval and the chip-security scandal add uncertainty to the AI investment cycle that has driven much of this year's stock market gains.

- Fed officials flag a possible half-point rate hike: A larger-than-expected hike would tighten financial conditions further, with direct implications for bond yields, the dollar and equity valuations already under pressure from the tech selloff.

- Euro slides to 18-month low on French political risk: A weaker euro driven by French political risk affects purchasing power and trade competitiveness across the eurozone, including Germany.
- ICJ to rule on Rohingya genocide case against Myanmar: A guilty verdict would set a landmark legal precedent for international accountability and could affect Myanmar's international standing and future reparations claims.
- Ukraine financing gap widens as allies meet and Crimea bridge is struck again: The financing gap and continued attacks on Crimea's infrastructure underscore the strain the war places on both Ukraine's finances and Russian logistics, with the EU loan a test of European unity.
