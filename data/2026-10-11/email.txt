Daily Briefing · Sunday, 11 October 2026

AT A GLANCE
- A strike on Riyadh's airport killed 12 and injured over 300; Trump is weighing whether the US joins Saudi strikes on Yemen's Houthis.

TOP STORIES
- Deadly strike on Riyadh airport deepens Yemen conflict: An escalating conflict near the Bab al-Mandab strait and Saudi energy infrastructure raises the risk of further disruption to Gulf shipping and oil markets, and could draw the US directly into the fighting.
- Trump says Ukraine needs new leader after diesel deal angers Zelenskyy: The rift exposes a split between Washington and Kyiv and European allies over Russia policy, with potential consequences for sanctions and transatlantic cooperation on Ukraine.
- India locks down Delhi to block protests against election chief: The scale of the crackdown in the world's largest democracy, ahead of state elections, highlights rising political tension around India's electoral process.
- Hurricane Isaias kills four, cuts power to 650,000 and disrupts US oil output: Storm-related disruption to Gulf Coast oil and gas output and the scale of insured losses could have knock-on effects for energy supply and insurers.
- US opens antitrust probe into networks that halted Trump pool coverage: The investigation raises questions about press access and coordination among major US broadcasters and could affect how the White House press pool operates going forward.
- RBI ramps up defense of rupee as it nears record lows: How far the RBI is willing to go to defend the rupee is a focus for trading this week, given India's status as the world's fastest-growing major economy.
- Nvidia weighs deeper investment in or acquisition of Reflection AI: A deal would deepen Nvidia's reach into AI model development and is framed by officials as part of US efforts to compete with Chinese AI offerings.
- AI debt issuance defies rising yields as SpaceX, Broadcom plan deals: A continued surge in AI-related corporate debt even as yields rise is a factor markets are watching for signs of stretched credit conditions in the sector.
- Apple cuts iPhone 18 Pro orders on soft demand: Reduced iPhone order volumes can signal weaker demand for Apple's latest device lineup, a watch point for its supply chain and sales outlook.
- Abbas postpones Palestinian elections to late 2027: The repeated postponement prolongs the absence of an electoral mandate for Palestinian leadership at a sensitive time for the region.
