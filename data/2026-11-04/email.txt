TOP STORIES
- Democrats sweep US midterms, take control of Congress: The result gives Democrats the power to block much of Trump's remaining legislative agenda and increases the likelihood of tighter scrutiny of the AI and tech industry, a shift markets reacted to immediately.
- Strait of Hormuz tensions escalate amid fears of renewed Iran war: Escalation in the Strait of Hormuz, through which a large share of global oil shipments pass, could disrupt energy markets and supply chains well beyond the region.
- North Korea fires missiles after denying role in $6.3bn Binance hack: The incident highlights security risks in crypto custody, and North Korea's missile tests add to tension with the US and Japan at a sensitive diplomatic moment.
- Trump says US will cut $3.8 billion in annual military aid to Israel: A reduction in US military aid would mark a shift in a decades-long pillar of US-Israel relations and could affect the security balance in the Middle East.
- Oracle cancels $7bn Tencent AI deal under Trump pressure: The cancellation signals growing US restrictions on AI-related business with China, adding friction for US tech firms with Chinese partners.
- AI-linked earnings diverge: AMD and Super Micro jump, Apple and Meta spend heavily: The mixed results show continued heavy capital spending on AI infrastructure even as some investors start questioning near-term returns.
- CVS Health moves to split its $117bn pharmacy and insurance empire: A breakup would reshape one of the largest US healthcare companies and could set a precedent for other vertically integrated health firms.
- Spain's transport minister resigns after deadly train crash: The resignation reflects political fallout from infrastructure safety failures and raises questions about oversight of Spain's rail network.
- Supermarket cyberattacks disrupt stores across Germany, UK and other EU countries: Repeated disruptions to grocery supply chains expose vulnerabilities in critical retail infrastructure across Europe, including in the reader's home market.
