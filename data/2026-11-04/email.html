Democrats sweep US midterms, take control of Congress
The result gives Democrats the power to block much of Trump's remaining legislative agenda and increases the likelihood of tighter scrutiny of the AI and tech industry, a shift markets reacted to immediately.

Strait of Hormuz tensions escalate amid fears of renewed Iran war
Escalation in the Strait of Hormuz, through which a large share of global oil shipments pass, could disrupt energy markets and supply chains well beyond the region.

North Korea fires missiles after denying role in $6.3bn Binance hack
The incident highlights security risks in crypto custody, and North Korea's missile tests add to tension with the US and Japan at a sensitive diplomatic moment.

Trump says US will cut $3.8 billion in annual military aid to Israel

Oracle cancels $7bn Tencent AI deal under Trump pressure
The cancellation signals growing US restrictions on AI-related business with China, adding friction for US tech firms with Chinese partners.

AI-linked earnings diverge: AMD and Super Micro jump, Apple and Meta spend heavily
