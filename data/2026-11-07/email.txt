TOP STORIES
- Wall Street's AI-driven selloff eases, but rebound called feeble: The scale of the pullback underscores how reliant equity markets have become on the AI investment theme, and a sustained pullback could ripple through financing for data centers and broader tech spending.
- Paraguay's president impeached and arrested after bank collapse: The sudden removal of a sitting president over a banking collapse highlights the risks of close ties between governments and politically connected lenders, and leaves millions of frozen depositors in limbo.
- Trump names immigration hardliner Stephen Miller as chief of staff: The choice of a hardline immigration figure to run the West Wing points to continued confrontation with the incoming Democratic Congress rather than a shift toward compromise.
- US strikes Iran's Qeshm island as diplomacy continues: Continued military strikes alongside active diplomacy point to an unresolved conflict that still threatens shipping and oil flows through a key global chokepoint.
- Coordinated bomb hoaxes disrupt European flights and newsrooms again: A sustained, coordinated hoax campaign adds to anxiety in Europe over hybrid attacks and strains aviation and media security resources even without any physical device being found.
- US presses China on Busan trade deal as sanctions dispute escalates: Renewed friction over the Busan deal's implementation threatens a fragile trade truce between the world's two largest economies, with rare earths supply a particular flashpoint for global manufacturers.
- US jobs report beats forecasts, reinforcing rate-hike expectations: A stronger-than-expected labor market reduces the odds of near-term rate cuts and could add pressure to the AI-linked stocks that had already been selling off on rate concerns.
- Tesla to end Cybertruck production, shift focus to Cybercab: The end of Cybertruck production marks a retreat from one of Musk's highest-profile consumer product bets and underscores investor doubts about parts of Tesla's growth story.
- AI data center backlash grows as financing strains emerge: Growing public resistance and signs of strain in data-center financing could slow the infrastructure buildout that has underpinned the AI investment boom.
- US believes Nvidia CEO had ties to alleged Chinese spy: The allegation touches the chief executive of the world's most valuable chipmaker at a moment of intense US-China tension over semiconductor technology and export controls.
