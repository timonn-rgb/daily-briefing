US inflation shock triggers global AI stock sell-off as Gulf tensions rise
Daily Briefing · Thursday, 15 October 2026

AT A GLANCE
- Hotter-than-expected US inflation data stoked Fed rate-hike fears, pulling the S&P 500 down 1.11% and the Nasdaq down 1.88% in their worst session since July.
- Chip-stock rout spread to Asia overnight: Japan's Nikkei fell 4.52%, Hang Seng dropped 1.47%, and headlines reported South Korea's Kospi sinking more than 7%.
- Iran escalated attacks in the Strait of Hormuz, sinking a container ship and striking Saudi Aramco's facilities overnight.
- Anthropic told investors third-quarter revenue hit $16.8 billion, up 50%, ahead of its IPO, though growth appears to be slowing.
- Student protests in Albania turned violent for a second night, with government buildings and banks set on fire in Tirana.
