Top stories

Trump strikes diesel deal with Putin, angering Ukraine
The deal eases Western sanctions pressure on Russia's energy sector and threatens to split Washington from Kyiv and European allies weeks before US midterm elections.

US sanctions the International Criminal Court
The sanctions deepen a rift between Washington and European allies over international justice institutions and could complicate transatlantic cooperation elsewhere.

Navi Pillay wins Nobel Peace Prize, Israel objects
The award keeps unresolved disputes over accountability for the Gaza war in the international spotlight just as the US moves to sanction the ICC.

Delhi locked down ahead of mass election protest
The standoff tests the independence of India's election machinery ahead of future votes in the world's most populous democracy.

Hurricane Isaias slams US Gulf Coast
The disruption to Gulf refining and production capacity adds fresh upward pressure on fuel prices just as Washington tries to bring them down.

7.7-magnitude earthquake strikes Panama
The quake underscores seismic risk across Central and South America and prompted a regional tsunami alert affecting several neighboring countries.

EU and China agree to halve hybrid car exports
The agreement eases a trade flashpoint that threatened European, including German, carmakers, while potentially reshaping competitive pressure in the auto industry.

Trump sets up new inquiry into Fed's Lisa Cook
The renewed push against Cook keeps the Federal Reserve's independence in question, a factor markets watch closely for its bearing on future interest-rate decisions.

SpaceX spectrum deal hits telecom stocks
A credible new entrant into US mobile service could pressure pricing and margins across the telecom sector for years.

Delta cuts 2026 forecast on fuel costs
As the first major carrier to report, Delta's warning signals that elevated fuel costs are squeezing airline profitability industry-wide heading into earnings season.
